Leo and Mia are at the park with their big dog, Barnaby. The sun is very hot, so they sit under a large, green tree. Suddenly, Barnaby finds a small, blue door in the grass!

Leo opens the door. Inside, they see flowers that can sing and a purple pond with orange fish. "Look!" shouts Mia. "The fish are wearing tiny hats!"

Leo and Mia drink pink lemonade and eat chocolate cookies. It is the best day ever.
